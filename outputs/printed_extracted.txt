Gender inequality defines the practice of granting
or denying rights or privileges, to the people based on
their gender. Despite the fact that women make more
than 50% of the world population, gender based dis-
crimination against women still remains an undeniable
fact in Pakistan and across the globe, potentially ham-
pering its socioeconomic advancement and progress.
The major half of the population is badly neglected in
almost all the fields of life, especially education,
health, economic opportunities and political participa-
tion.
